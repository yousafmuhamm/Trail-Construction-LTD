# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners, builders, and property owners in Calgary, Olds, and south central Alberta who need structural carpentry or related site work. Most arrive on mobile, often through a referral or shared link, and need to quickly understand the company’s capabilities, see representative work, and call or request a quote.

## Product Purpose

Present Trail Construction Ltd. as an experienced, owner-operated structural contractor with a clear emphasis on bearing-wall removals and beam installations. Success means visitors can identify the right service, inspect credible work photos and plain-language details, learn who Ken is, and make contact without friction.

## Brand Personality

Practical, experienced, and straightforward. The voice should sound like a capable tradesperson explaining the work clearly: confident without boasting, helpful without pressure, and honest about scope, timing, and fit.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic contractor-template styling, fabricated project specifics, glossy luxury-home marketing, corporate construction jargon, and sales-heavy copy. The site should not bury useful information behind decorative effects or make secondary capabilities appear equal to the company’s core structural work.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the work Trail does most and best.
2. Show the work and explain it in Ken’s plain voice.
3. Make calling and requesting a quote effortless on mobile.
4. Use hierarchy to distinguish flagship, main, and secondary services.
5. Be honest about what is confirmed, what is representative, and where Trail may not be the right fit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, comfortable 44px touch targets, useful image alternatives, keyboard-operable dialogs and galleries, and reduced-motion support. Mobile layouts at 375px are a primary requirement rather than a fallback.
